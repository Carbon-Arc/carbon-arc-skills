# Carbon Arc Location Intelligence

A report on a place you can send on: **how a metro area's economy is doing against comparable metro
areas**, **what a dated shock did to it**, such as a storm, a plant closure or a major opening, or **how a
venue is doing against comparable venues**. It reads
the place from two sides: what the people who live there spend and on what, and what happens at its
locations.

| | Skill | The question |
|---|---|---|
| | `carbon-arc-onboarding` | Start here. What this is, which plugin and report you want, and how to begin. |
| | `location-report` | How is this place doing? One report covering the four workflows below. |
| | `venue-report` | How is this venue doing? Its ticket market, the shows and games behind it, the blocks around it, and who its crowds also buy for. |

**In the place report**

| | Skill | The question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `loc-residents` | How are the people who live here spending? |
| 2 | `loc-spending-mix` | What are residents spending more and less on? |
| 3 | `loc-places` | Are people coming to the places here, and how long did a slump last? |
| 4 | `loc-building` | How much is being built or repaired, and is it more than usual? |

**In the venue report**

| | Skill | The question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `loc-venue` | How is the venue's ticket market doing, what drives it, do event days reach the blocks around it, and who do its crowds also buy for? |
| 2 | `loc-places` | Are people coming to the blocks around the venue? (read at its ZIP code) |
| 3 | `loc-residents` | How are the people who live around the venue spending? (read at its ZIP code) |

**What it reads against:** three to five comparable metro areas, chosen from public records so that the
comparison is to places an event did not reach, and published local figures such as lodging tax where you
have them.

**What it cannot see:** what visitors spend inside the place, hotel occupancy, unemployment, population and
home prices at metro grain, closures of individual businesses, and for a venue, attendance and sell-outs
(every ticket figure is resale).
