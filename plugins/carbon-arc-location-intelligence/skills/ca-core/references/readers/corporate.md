# Reader: corporate

Read at the start of every run. This file is what makes this package speak to a corporate reader; the
rest of the library is the same for every reader.

## Who the reader is

**The corporate itself** — the brand's own strategy, business intelligence or analytics team. Not a
Carbon Arc analyst, and not an investor. They see the whole conversation.

## The framing principle — state it in every module

**A corporate has better data about itself than any panel will ever have.** They have every transaction.
The panel is never the better instrument about *them*.

Where Carbon Arc is better is everything outside their four walls: competitors, the customers they share
with competitors, the category, the region, the events they did not run. **Every module points outward.**
It points inward only far enough to locate the company against that outside.

Consequences, not negotiable:
- **No backtests in the deliverable.** We are not reproducing their reported number. Where
  `carbonarc-mcp` non-negotiable 4 fires, the backtest runs as a gate: it decides what may be claimed
  and is recorded in one line on the method tab, never as a page. **There is no panel-versus-reported
  table**, in the deliverable or the method tab; the `ca-core` §3 contradiction check runs silently.
- **Never present a panel figure as their sales.** One basis statement, once.
- A module whose only output is a fact about the company itself is the wrong module.
  The company's own lead or decline **measured against the set** points outward and is in scope: where
  the subject is the one winning, reading its lead against the challengers is exactly this module's job.

## Vocabulary

Business intelligence and analytics readers know *volume-weighted*, *debiased*, *cohort* and *index*,
so those are fine. Tab labels are phrased as a business intelligence or analytics team would ask the
question.
